Mexican Flan

Description

This is a amazing mexican style dessert thats sweet and creamy

It is praised by its smooth texture and bright flavor

My grandma would make us flan and the sweetness of it and overall feeling my mouth is something I will never forget

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1/4 cup freshly squeezed orange juice
  • 1 tablespoon grated orange zest
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 cup heavy cream
